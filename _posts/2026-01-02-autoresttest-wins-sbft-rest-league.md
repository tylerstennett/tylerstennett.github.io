---
title: "AutoRestTest wins the SBFT REST League 🎉"
---

AutoRestTest took first place in all three categories of the [SBFT REST League](https://github.com/SeUniVr/RestLeague) tool competition: fault detection, overall efficiency, and overall effectiveness. Big thanks to the organizers for putting together a great competition, and congrats to all the participating teams. The tool is [open source](https://github.com/selab-gatech/AutoRestTest) if you want to take a look.
